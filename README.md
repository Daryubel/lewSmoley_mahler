## Chinese term annotations

The Chinese editions keep the Chinese translation in the main text and add the original
English/German/Latin form in parentheses on the first occurrence of dedicated names and
titles: people (`布鲁诺·瓦尔特（Bruno Walter）`), works
(`《大地之歌》（Das Lied von der Erde）`), movement names (`炼狱（Purgatorio）`), and
programme titles (`“巨人”（Titan）`, `“少年时代”（From the Days of Youth）`).
`Mahler` and ordinary analytical vocabulary are not annotated.

## Source

Texts are based on the corrected subtitles in the project root; the articles have been
punctuated and paragraphed for reading. PDFs use XeLaTeX with an academic A4 layout;
EPUBs use a simple serif stylesheet.
